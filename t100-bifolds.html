Why T100 Bifolds?

T100 Bifolds stand out for their robust engineering, smooth top-hung system, and adaptability to both aluminium and timber doors. Engineered for strength and seamless operation, they support heavy panels while providing effortless folding performance. With versatile design options and proven durability for both residential and commercial spaces, T100 Bifolds offer reliability, style, and flexibility, making them an ideal choice for those who demand more from their door systems.

Key Features & Benefits

The T100 Bifolds offer a range of key features and benefits that set them apart in the market. With a robust top-hung system, they ensure smooth and reliable folding action, even with heavy panels up to 100kg. Their 2-way adaptability makes them suitable for both aluminium and timber doors, catering to various design needs. Engineered for durability and consistent performance, T100 Bifolds seamlessly suit residential and commercial spaces, providing long-lasting security, style, and flexible integration for modern architectural projects.

How TITAN Doorware Integrates

TITAN Doorware integrates seamlessly with the T100 Bifolds, offering a perfect match of hardware and folding door system engineered for reliable, long-lasting performance. The compatibility of TITAN components ensures smooth operation and precise alignment, whether the panels are aluminium or timber. With a focus on quality and durability, TITAN Doorware enhances the overall function and security of the T100 Bifolds, allowing these systems to meet the diverse needs of both residential and commercial projects while maintaining a cohesive and stylish look.

Engineered for High-Capacity Folding Panels

Titan Doorware is designed to meet the rigorous demands of the T100 Bifold system, accommodating panels up to 100kg. This makes it an ideal hardware solution for both aluminum and timber applications requiring robust, reliable performance in top-hung folding door configurations.

Compatible Hardware for Diverse Applications

The integration of Titan Doorware with T100 Bifold systems ensures seamless operation in both residential and commercial spaces. Hardware components are compatible with a variety of panel materials, supporting the versatile use of T100 bifolds in different architectural projects.

Precision Installation with Secure Top-Hung Support

Titan hardware is precisely engineered to align with T100 Bifold tracks and carriers. The result is a straightforward installation process, providing secure top-hung support that guarantees smooth and consistent folding action for heavy doors.

Long-Lasting Durability for Demanding Environments

Built for reliability, Titan Doorware enhances the durability of T100 Bifold doors, ensuring that door systems remain functional and secure even under frequent use. The high-quality materials and corrosion-resistant finishes are ideal for demanding environments, both indoors and outdoors.
