Why T100 Bifolds?

T100 Bifolds stand out for their robust engineering, smooth top-hung system, and adaptability to both aluminium and timber doors. Engineered for strength and seamless operation, they support heavy panels while providing effortless folding performance. With versatile design options and proven durability for both residential and commercial spaces, T100 Bifolds offer reliability, style, and flexibility, making them an ideal choice for those who demand more from their door systems.

Key Features & Benefits

The T100 Bifolds offer a range of key features and benefits that set them apart in the market. With a robust top-hung system, they ensure smooth and reliable folding action, even with heavy panels up to 100kg. Their 2-way adaptability makes them suitable for both aluminium and timber doors, catering to various design needs. Engineered for durability and consistent performance, T100 Bifolds seamlessly suit residential and commercial spaces, providing long-lasting security, style, and flexible integration for modern architectural projects.
