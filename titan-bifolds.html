Folding Motion

TITAN hardware is meticulously engineered to deliver smooth and reliable folding motion in bifold door systems. Utilizing advanced components and precision design, TITAN’s folding solutions allow door panels to operate effortlessly along the track, enabling wide openings and seamless transitions between spaces. This innovative folding mechanism ensures panels move in perfect alignment with minimal resistance, even under substantial weight. By protecting the mechanism from debris and wear, TITAN hardware guarantees lasting performance and reduces maintenance. The result is a superior folding experience—combining strength, convenience, and modern aesthetics—that’s ideal for both residential and commercial projects.
