Top Hung Motion

TITAN hardware is specifically engineered to deliver superior top hung motion in sliding and folding door systems. By suspending panels from an overhead track, TITAN’s top hung solutions ensure doors glide smoothly and quietly with minimal floor resistance. This design not only provides effortless operation, even with heavier panels, but also protects the sliding mechanism from debris and reduces maintenance needs. The precision components and advanced roller technology in TITAN hardware contribute to long-lasting reliability and a premium user experience, making it the ideal choice for projects that demand both performance and elegance.
