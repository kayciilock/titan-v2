Why T350 Sliders?

T350 Sliders stand out for their robust engineering, smooth sliding mechanism, and compatibility with both aluminium and timber doors. Engineered for strength and easy operation, they support large, heavy panels while delivering effortless gliding performance. With versatile design options and proven durability for both residential and commercial spaces, T350 Sliders offer reliability, style, and flexibility, making them an ideal choice for those who want to seamlessly connect their indoor and outdoor environments.

Key Features & Benefits

The T350 Sliders offer a range of key features and benefits that set them apart in the market. Engineered for durability and consistent performance, T350 Sliders seamlessly suit residential and commercial spaces, providing lasting security, style, and effortless integration into modern architectural projects.
