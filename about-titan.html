About TITAN

TITAN is a leader in doorware innovation, renowned for engineering high-quality solutions that combine durability, style, and reliable performance. Revolution
Simple One Click Install

With no unsightly screw threads, springs or locking mechanisms visible once installed, nothing remains to detract focus from the wide and seamless view of the surroundings beyond. Suitable for both residential and commercial projects, this “Be-All and End-All” systems’ robust 100kg load rating makes it ideal for use alongside large, heavy duty glasspanels. Drawing inspiration from the efficient and robust design of pneumatic quick couplers—devices celebrated for their ability to quickly and safely establish secure, high-pressure connections we've reimagined bifold door installation.

Innovation
Quick Fix Pin

Carriage geometry, track profile and adjustment points were developed as a single system, not as isolated components. The result is a hardware package that can accept building movement and fabrication variance without losing control of the slide.

Innovating the Bi-Fold Experience
Design Journey

Bi-Fold doors are celebrated for seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor spaces, adaptable to any lifestyle or aesthetic. Recognising the challenges installers often face, the Titan system was crafted with innovative features that deliver smooth, intuitive operation for users while simplifying installation — setting a new standard in performance and efficiency.

Security Meets Convenience
Unique Locking System with Retractable Key

Our unique locking system features a retractable key design that combines enhanced security with user-friendly operation. Engineered for smooth engagement and discreet aesthetics, this system ensures reliable locking while providing easy access, making it the ideal choice for modern door hardware solutions.
